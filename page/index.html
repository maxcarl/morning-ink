Morning Ink

Your feeds, newsletters and local news, set as a newspaper and waiting on your Kindle when you wake up.

Morning Ink runs on your own server. Every morning it collects what you follow, takes out the clutter, builds an EPUB with cover and sections, and mails it to your e-reader.

While you sleep

You pick the sources and the delivery time once. Morning Ink starts building as far ahead as you set, so the issue goes out on the minute.

  1. 5:00

    Collect

    Feeds, FreshRSS, newsletters from your mailbox, Hacker News, Reddit and the news from your town.

  2. 5:10

    Read in full

    The whole article, not the teaser. Ads, live tickers and cookie notices stay out.

  3. 5:20

    Sort

    Your filters and interests decide what goes in. The same story from three sources becomes one.

  4. 5:30

    Summarize

    If you want it: an overview of the most important things, from your own Ollama or any provider.

  5. 5:45

    Set

    An EPUB with cover, contents and sections, images sized for e-ink.

  6. 6:00

    Deliver

    By mail to your Kindle. KOReader and other OPDS readers fetch it themselves.

What it does

A newspaper you put together yourself, on a server you own.

Your newspaper

Any feed, in full text
RSS and Atom, cleaned up for e-ink. OPML import to bring your feeds along.
Newsletters
Read over IMAP, read only. Nothing in your mailbox is moved or deleted.
Local news and weather
For your town, with the articles themselves, not just headlines.
Several editions
Morning, evening, weekend, each with its own sections, time and weekdays.

Your control

Filters per source
Words to require or exclude, a minimum length, no tickers, no marked ads.
Feedback from the Kindle
“More of this” and “Less of this” under every article move a source up or down.
Archive
Search old issues, download them, send them again.
Optional AI
Summaries, a five-minute overview and plain headlines. Private sources can stay on your own machine.

Your server

One container
SQLite and a built-in scheduler. No database server, no queue, amd64 and arm64.
Family and friends
Each user with their own sources, editions and Kindle.
Safe by default
Blocks requests into your home network, encrypts stored passwords, cleans all HTML.
Notifications
ntfy, Gotify, Telegram or e-mail when an issue is out, or when it is not.

The newsroom

The web interface looks like what it makes. The next issue, a preview, and every past issue with its delivery status.

Works without AI AI only adds extras. When no model is reachable, the newspaper still comes out on time.

Your first issue in ten minutes

On a NAS, a Raspberry Pi 4 or 5, or any computer that is always on.

  1. Start the container

    Save this as compose.yaml in an empty folder.

    services:
  morning-ink:
    image: ghcr.io/maxcarl/morning-ink:latest
    ports:
      - "8080:8080"
    volumes:
      - morning-ink-data:/data
    restart: unless-stopped

volumes:
  morning-ink-data:
    docker compose up -d
docker compose logs morning-ink | grep "setup code"

  2. Create your account

    Open port 8080 in your browser and enter the setup code from the log. It proves you own this installation.

  3. Follow the wizard

    Your Send-to-Kindle address, your mail server, your town and a few sample sources. Then send the first issue.

Before the first issue: add your sender address to Amazon's approved list under Personal Document Settings. Amazon drops mail from unknown senders without telling anyone.

Morning Ink is for your own reading. Full texts and archived issues are for personal use; paid content only works with your own subscription.
